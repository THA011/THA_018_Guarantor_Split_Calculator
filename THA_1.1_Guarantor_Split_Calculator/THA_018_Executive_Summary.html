01 The Problem

A recurring appraisal problem, standardized.

When a member's loan needs guaranteeing, it's common to have several guarantors on file with very different available deposit balances. Deciding how much of the guarantee requirement each one should carry is a judgment call — and one that, made by hand, doesn't hold up well under scrutiny or repetition.

In day-to-day credit operations, that manual approach shows up as four consistent issues:

Inconsistent — varies by officer, by day Slow — re-derived per application Hard to defend — no documented method Risky at the edges — can over-expose small savers

02 The Solution

THA_1.1 is a free, single-file browser tool that removes the guesswork. A Credit Officer enters the required guarantee amount and each guarantor's available deposit, and the engine instantly computes two transparent, defensible split methods side by side.

03 How It Works

The engine offers two fair, explainable methods — the choice between them depends on whether the guarantor group prioritizes equal responsibility or proportional exposure.

Method 1 — Equal Capped (Water-Filling)

Everyone contributes an equal share, capped at what they actually have.

  1. Divide the requirement equally among all active guarantors.
  2. Anyone below that equal share contributes their full deposit and exits the pool.
  3. The remaining requirement re-divides equally among the rest.
  4. Repeat until every remaining share fits within its guarantor's deposit.

Method 2 — Proportional (Weighted)

Each guarantor pledges the same percentage of their own balance.

Share = ( Deposit ÷ Total Deposits ) × Loan Amount

Protects small savers from disproportionate exposure — a large-balance guarantor absorbs more KES, but the same percentage of their own savings as everyone else.
